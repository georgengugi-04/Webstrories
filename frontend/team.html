Who's Behind It

Meet the Newsroom

GreenTrack began in June 2026 as a collaboration between our team at KCA University and Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT), together with JHUB Africa.

GreenTrack started as a student attachment project — a small team that decided a traceability app for Kenyan agriculture was worth building properly, not just presenting. What's on this site is the output of that team: the product, the platform, and the reporting in the Journal.
